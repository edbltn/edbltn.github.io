Settings

Wings

Web embed

A slide left empty in the deck becomes a live page. Point a particular slide at its own page in embeds.json, or put embed: https://… in that slide's speaker notes. Zooming out gives the frame a bigger viewport and scales it down, so you see more of the page. Preload loads a page while its slide is still that many slides away, so it is ready when you arrive — at the cost of the page being open early. It takes the mouse so you can drive it; v hands clicks back to the deck.

Video

Video comes from the deck itself — an embedded clip, or a slide linked to YouTube. Sound needs a gesture, so the first clip may start muted; m toggles sound.

Downloads each linked clip with yt-dlp and plays it as a plain video — no player chrome, no network during the talk. Needs brew install yt-dlp.

Layout

Detection

Higher = fewer pages merged into one slide. Manual overrides in the structure inspector always win.

Structure

Each junction between two pages is either a rotation (a new slide, panes move across the triptych) or a build (a step inside the current slide). Click a junction to flip it.
