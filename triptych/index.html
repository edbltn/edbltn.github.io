Web embed

A slide left empty in the deck becomes a live page. Point a particular slide at its own page in embeds.json, or put embed: https://… in that slide's speaker notes. Zooming out gives the frame a bigger viewport and scales it down, so you see more of the page. Preload loads a page while its slide is still that many slides away, so it is ready when you arrive — at the cost of the page being open early. It takes the mouse so you can drive it; v hands clicks back to the deck.
